# Privacy Policy

## The waitlist (truetone.pages.dev)

If you join the early-access list, we collect **your email address** and your confirmation that
you are 18 or older and in the United States. That is everything. We do not collect your name,
your location, or anything about your skin — joining the list involves no photo and no scan.

**Purpose:** to email you once, when there is a build you can use.

**Retention:** we delete your address 90 days after we send you that invite, or 3 years after you
join if we never do, whichever comes first. An automated job enforces this — it is not a promise
we have to remember to keep.

**Unsubscribe:** every email carries a one-tap unsubscribe link, which deletes your address
outright rather than flagging it as inactive.

We do not sell, lease, trade, or share your email address. The waitlist page loads no analytics,
no advertising pixel, and no third-party script of any kind.

## The app

What we collect: face image (processed on-device, never uploaded), derived skin-appearance
metrics, and account information. Specific purpose: estimate your skin's appearance and suggest a
cosmetic routine.

We never sell, lease, or trade biometric or health data. We do not share it with advertisers or
analytics providers. You can view and delete your data in-app at any time.
