skin-appearance score **bands**, skin-type label, cosmetic routine, and the chat messages you submit
so Anthropic can generate the response you requested. Anthropic does not receive your face photo or
your raw numeric scan scores. TrueTone does not save the chat transcript in its database; the chat
screen keeps it only for the current session. Do not enter information in chat that you do not want
processed for this purpose.
