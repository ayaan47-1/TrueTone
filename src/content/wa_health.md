# Washington Consumer Health Data Policy

This standalone policy applies to Washington residents under the My Health My Data Act (MHMDA).

**Consumer health data we collect:** a photo of your face and derived skin-appearance metrics.
**Purpose:** to estimate your skin's appearance and suggest a cosmetic routine.
**Consent:** we collect this data only after your explicit, affirmative consent.
**Sharing/sale:** we do not sell your consumer health data. If you separately choose this feature,
TrueTone's backend sends **Anthropic** your derived skin-appearance score **bands**, skin-type label,
cosmetic routine, and the chat messages you submit so Anthropic can generate the response you
requested. Anthropic does not receive your face photo or your raw numeric scan scores. TrueTone does
not save the chat transcript in its database; the chat screen keeps it only for the current session.
Do not enter information in chat that you do not want processed for this purpose.
**Your rights:** you may withdraw consent, view, and delete your data in-app at any time.
