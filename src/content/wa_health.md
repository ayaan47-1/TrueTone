# Washington Consumer Health Data Policy

This standalone policy applies to Washington residents under the My Health My Data Act (MHMDA).

**Consumer health data we collect:** a photo of your face and derived skin-appearance metrics.
**Purpose:** to estimate your skin's appearance and suggest a cosmetic routine.
**Consent:** we collect this data only after your explicit, affirmative consent.
**Sharing/sale:** we do not sell or share your consumer health data. We would never do so without a
separate signed authorization.
**Ingredient flags (optional).** If you choose to list cosmetic ingredients for us to flag:
- *Category:* the cosmetic ingredient names you ask us to flag.
- *Purpose:* only to flag those ingredients when we show you products and build routines. Never
  for advertising, analytics or model training.
- *Source:* you.
- *Sharing:* no one. We never share or sell them.
- *Storage:* encrypted, only on this phone. They are not sent to our servers.
- *Your controls:* view, edit or delete them anytime in Account → Ingredient flags; withdrawing
  consent or deleting your data removes them. We keep only a receipt that you consented.

**Your rights:** you may withdraw consent, view, and delete your data in-app at any time.
