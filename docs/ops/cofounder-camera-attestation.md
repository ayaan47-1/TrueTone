# TrueTone — On-Device Camera Build: Principal Testing Attestation

**Purpose.** This attestation records that a second company principal is knowingly
participating in internal testing ("dogfooding") of a pre-release TrueTone build in
which the on-device camera / shade-match feature is enabled. It is **not** a consent
form for collecting a member of the public's biometric data — it documents a
company principal testing **his own** biometric data on **his own** device.

Reference: `docs/ops/camera-on-testflight-compliance.md` §12 (standing two-principal
dogfooding policy).

I, the undersigned, attest that:

1. I am a **principal of the Company** (co-founder / officer), not a member of the
   general public.

2. I am **knowingly and voluntarily** testing a pre-release TrueTone build that has
   the live on-device camera / shade-match feature enabled, for internal product
   development purposes.

3. The face image I capture is **my own biometric data**, captured on **my own
   personal device**.

4. I understand that the image is **processed entirely on-device and deleted
   immediately after processing**, and that **nothing** — no image, no facial
   geometry, no derived template, and no consent record — is transmitted to, stored
   by, or retained by the Company or any server. Only non-identifying,
   on-device-derived cosmetic descriptors (e.g., a shade name) are used, and the
   underlying image does not leave my device.

5. I understand I completed the in-app age and consent screens, and I **consent** to
   this on-device capture and processing of my own biometric data for internal
   testing.

6. I understand this arrangement is limited to the **two named company principals
   only**, that the build is delivered to my device by **direct install (not
   TestFlight / not the App Store)**, and that I may stop testing and delete the
   build at any time.
