# Biometric Data Policy

**What we collect (BIPA §15(b)):** To analyze your skin's appearance, TrueTone captures a photo of
your face and measures visible features of your skin (a "biometric identifier" under laws like
Illinois' BIPA and a "consumer health datum" under Washington's My Health My Data Act).

**Purpose:** To estimate your skin's appearance and suggest a cosmetic routine. We use it for
nothing else.

**Retention & destruction:** We delete your biometric data when its purpose is met or within 3
years of your last interaction, whichever comes first — and immediately whenever you tap
"Delete My Data."

**We never sell, lease, or trade this data, and never share it with third parties for advertising.**
