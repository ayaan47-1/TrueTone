A few things to know.

Is TrueTone available yet?

TrueTone is in development. Join the waitlist for early-access updates. The initial app is intended for people 18 or older in the United States, starting on iPhone.

Which brands are you interested in?

We’re open to conversations with skincare and makeup brands with products available in the United States. Assortment, product information, and commercial arrangements are discussed together.

Does a partnership guarantee a listing?

No. We’re in early discussions. Listings, timing, and commercial terms are agreed individually. A partnership does not buy a place in independent recommendation rankings or guarantee sales.

Do brands receive scan information?

No. Face images, scan scores, and scan-based targeting segments are kept away from brands and advertising partners. Photos stay on the phone and are deleted after the read. This website does not collect photos or skin data.
