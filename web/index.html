Things we'd rather say now than later

What TrueTone is not.

  • Not a diagnosis. TrueTone describes appearance only. It can't tell you whether something is a medical condition, and it won't try. Ask it about a mole or a lesion and it will tell you to see a dermatologist — every time.
  • Not a replacement for a dermatologist. If something on your skin is changing, painful, or worrying you, that's a doctor's job.
  • Not tested across every skin tone yet. Working equally well from Fitzpatrick I to VI is the point of the product, and we're building it that way — but we won't claim it's proven until we have the data to back it up.
  • Not available everywhere yet. United States only for the first release, and 18 or older.
